Small tools I use to keep up with the literature, built from my bibliography scripts.
Find new papers on my research topics, read their abstracts, then turn the ones worth keeping into BibTeX and tidy the .bib file.
Add up a differential privacy budget.
And see when the next security and machine learning conferences take place and how long is left to submit.
