abstract: Image-based malware classification faces three practical problems — limited datasets, imbalanced datasets, and poor generalizability of the trained models. To tackle the limited-data problem, this work uses a prototypical-network-based few-shot learning approach, achieving 98.71% accuracy while training on only four samples per class, and further addresses class imbalance and generalization to unknown malware families.
